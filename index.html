Introductions

Hello all!

If you somehow didn’t catch this, I am causticsalsa, and this is the site for more information on my AO3 fic. Yes, I felt the need to publish a site, don’t judge me. I just knew that not everything I wrote would make it into the final fic, so I wanted to give it a place to live.

Things on this site will be updated pretty often, so don’t worry about random inconsistencies or things that changed from the last time you saw them. If anything, this gives you a little insight into my psychotic writing process. Take that how you will!

I would NOT suggest reading extensively if you aren't caught up on the fic. I will do my best to keep it updated to where the fic is to avoid giving any spoilers, but currently 90% of this lives up in my noggin.

Part of the purpose of doing this is to also convince myself that all this worldbuilding is worth it, so if you're interested, give it a read! I don’t know how long I’ll keep this up, but at least while I’m uploading. However long that lasts...

Best of luck!
